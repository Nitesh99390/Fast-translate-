Help Center

Questions, answered.

Which file formats work?

EPUB, PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, HTML, HTM, and XHTML. You can also convert between them while translating.

How fast is translation?

A typical full-length novel takes about 4–6 minutes. Shorter files finish even quicker, and Premium users jump straight to the priority queue.

Is formatting preserved?

Yes. Bold, italics, headings, links, cover images, tables of contents, CSS styles, and chapter structure carry over cleanly.

Why is there a 200 files / month limit on subscriptions?

200 files a month equals 6–7 full books every single day. This fair usage policy prevents server abuse so every genuine reader enjoys fast, uninterrupted translations without higher subscription costs.

How does the free plan work?

You get 1,000,000 characters every day with files up to 2 MB each—enough for multiple books a day, free forever.

What happens to my uploaded files?

Files are processed in memory/temporary storage and deleted immediately after delivery. We never store your books.

Do subscriptions auto-renew?

No. Subscriptions never auto-renew, so you stay in full control. Credit packs never expire and are only used after your daily free quota runs out.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If you’re not satisfied within 7 days, reach out for a full refund. Failed translations never consume your credits or quota.
